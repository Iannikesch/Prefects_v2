How a stranger becomes a customer

Five stages, in order. And proof that it happened.

01 · Recognize

Find out who's actually visiting, buying and opening.

We match visits, emails and purchases to real people in our database. Most of your traffic stops being anonymous.

02 · Unify

One record per customer, across every system you run.

Nonna's Table had three locations with three guest lists. Now each guest is one record.

03 · Understand

Know who's worth the most, and who's about to leave.

Scoring built on your own sales history, not industry averages.

04 · Decide

Send the right message without a person pressing send.

AI chooses the offer, the channel and the time for each customer.

05 · Prove

See which dollar drove which sale.

Incrementality tests, so you're measuring what changed because of us, not what would have happened anyway.
